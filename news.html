No. 01 — UNITED STATES · SINGAPORE

Washington Blacklists Chip Firm in Nvidia Espionage Case

The United States has blacklisted Singapore's Megaspeed, accused of funnelling advanced Nvidia chips to China.

The Commerce Department moved against Megaspeed on Monday, placing the Singapore chip firm on its entity list over allegations that it served as a conduit for restricted Nvidia processors bound for China.

The listing cuts the company off from American technology and marks the sharpest escalation yet in Washington's campaign against the smuggling networks that have kept advanced AI hardware flowing despite export controls.

Investigators describe a chain of intermediaries across Southeast Asia. The case now tests how far enforcement can reach beyond America's own borders — and how much of the trade simply moves elsewhere.
